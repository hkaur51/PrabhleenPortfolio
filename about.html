Hi, I’m Prabhleen. I grew up in India and moved to New York for Parsons, where my interest in fashion became much more focused on how brands actually speak to people.

I studied Fashion Media & Communication before earning my master’s in Fashion Management at Parsons. Since then, I’ve worked across fashion, jewelry, communications, creative projects and luxury resale - and I’ve realized that the part I enjoy most is turning a product or an idea into something people want to stop and look at.

I’m especially drawn to fashion and jewelry because they sit somewhere between product and identity. I like work that feels polished without feeling too perfect: strong imagery, a clear point of view and ideas that make sense for how people actually consume content.

I also genuinely enjoy the messy part before the finished work - pulling references, noticing trends, building a concept and obsessing over the small details until everything feels right.
